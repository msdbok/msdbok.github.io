# Revision Questions

Question sets for working through a lecture after you have read it. Each page holds
**lecture questions**, which check that you can restate and apply what the session argued,
and **reading questions**, which go through the assigned papers and books one at a time.

No answers are published. The questions are written so that a good answer is a short
paragraph making a claim and supporting it, which is also how the quizzes are marked: state
your position first, then give the reasoning or the evidence for it.

- [RQ1 — People](rq1.html) covers personality, culture, communication, motivation, leadership, teams, psychological safety, social loafing and decision making.
- [RQ4 — Scope](rq4.html) covers the work breakdown structure and estimation.
- [RQ5 — Planning](rq5.html) covers planning, activities and milestones.

Study notes for the same material are under [Study Notes](notes.html).
