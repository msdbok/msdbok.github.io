# Planning

A plan is a set of documented agreements on what will be delivered, when, and by whom
{% cite miranda_managing_2014 %}. This area runs from the commitments a sponsor sees down to the
tasks a team schedules, in the order they are usually made. How progress is measured against the
plan belongs to [Tracking](../track/).

- **[Planning approaches](approaches)**: why teams plan, and how predictive and agile plans differ.
- **[Milestone planning](milestones)**: results a sponsor can check, and how they get their dates.
- **[Activity planning](activities)**: turning milestones into tasks and dependencies.
- **[Critical path](critical-path)**: which tasks set the end date, and how much the others can slip.
- **[Gantt charts](gantt)**: the plan on a calendar, and what the chart hides.
- **[PERT and merge bias](pert)**: three-point estimates, and why parallel paths finish late.
- **[Design Structure Matrix](dsm)**: work whose information loops back.
- **[Critical chain](chain)**: scheduling around scarce people with pooled buffers.
- **[Resource planning](resources)**: the hours that exist, levelling, and adding people late.
- **[Release planning](release/)**: choosing what goes into each release when the date is fixed.
